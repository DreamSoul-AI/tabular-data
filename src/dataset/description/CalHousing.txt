Dataset Information
The California Housing Prices dataset is based on the 1990 U.S. Census and provides information on housing prices and related statistics for districts in California. This dataset serves as an introductory dataset for machine learning tasks, particularly regression. The task is to predict the median house value based on various features related to housing and demographic information.

There are 10 features in total, 9 of which are input features and 1 is the target variable.

Additional Variable Information
Input variables:
	1 - longitude: A measure of how far west a house is; a higher value indicates farther west (numeric)
	2 - latitude: A measure of how far north a house is; a higher value indicates farther north (numeric)
    3 - housing_median_age: Median age of a house within a district (numeric)
	4 - total_rooms: Total number of rooms within a district (numeric)
	5 - total_bedrooms: Total number of bedrooms within a district (numeric)
	6 -  population: Total number of people residing within a district (numeric)
	7 - households: Total number of households (numeric)
	8 - median_income: Median income for households within a district (in tens of thousands of US Dollars)
	10 - ocean_proximity: Location of the house relative to the ocean (categorical: 'NEAR BAY', 'NEAR OCEAN', 'INLAND', 'ISLAND', 'LATIN AMERICA')
Output variable (desired target):
	9 - median_house_value: Median house value for households within a district (US Dollars)
