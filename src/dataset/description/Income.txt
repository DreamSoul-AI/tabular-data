Adult Census Income  
Donated on 4/30/1996  
Predict whether the annual income of an individual exceeds $50K/yr based on census data. Also known as "Census Income" dataset.

### Dataset Information
The data was extracted from the 1994 Census database by Ronny Kohavi and Barry Becker. A set of reasonably clean records was extracted using the following conditions: ((AAGE>16) && (AGI>100) && (AFNLWGT>1) && (HRSWK>0)). The goal is to predict whether a person's income exceeds $50,000 per year. This dataset includes demographic attributes, education, work-related features, and income-related information.

Has Missing Values?

Introductory Paper
