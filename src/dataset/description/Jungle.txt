### Dataset Information  
This dataset contains tablebases for various two-piece endgames in Jungle Chess, with positions for pieces like Rat, Panther, Lion, and Elephant. The dataset focuses on predicting the outcome (win/loss/draw) from the configuration of two pieces on the board. The dataset contains several subproblems with similar positions, which can aid in classification tasks related to endgame analysis.
