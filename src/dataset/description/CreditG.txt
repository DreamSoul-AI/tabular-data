Credit Risk Customers
Donated on 2022
The dataset classifies individuals as good or bad credit risks based on 20 features. The goal is to predict whether a customer can be given credit, with a binary classification outcome (good/bad credit risk). The dataset is well-suited for practicing data cleaning, feature engineering, and building binary classification models.

Dataset Information
This dataset is used to classify people as good or bad credit risks based on various attributes such as checking account status, credit history, purpose of the credit, and personal data. The dataset contains several features that require data cleaning, making it an excellent choice for practicing data preprocessing and feature engineering.

Additional Variable Information
Input variables:
   1. checking_status: Status of the existing checking account  
      - Categorical: "no checking", "<0", "0<=X<200", ">=200"
   2. duration: Duration of the credit  
      - Numeric, in months
   3. credit_history: History of credit  
      - Categorical: "critical/other existing credit", "existing paid", "delayed previously", "no credits/all paid", "all paid", "none"
   4. purpose: Purpose of the credit  
      - Categorical: "radio/tv", "education", "furniture/equipment", "new car", "used car", "business", "domestic appliance", "repairs"
   5. credit_amount: Amount of credit requested  
      - Numeric, in currency
   6. savings_status: Savings account/bond status  
      - Categorical: "little", "moderate", "rich", "none"
   7. employment: Employment duration  
      - Numeric, in years
   8. installment_commitment: Installment commitment as percentage of disposable income  
      - Numeric
   9. personal_status: Sex and marital status  
      - Categorical: "male single", "male div/sep", "male mar/wid", "female div/sep", "female mar/wid", "female single"
   10. other_parties: Other debtors/guarantors  
       - Categorical: "none", "guarantor", "co applicant"
Target variable:
   11. y: Credit risk classification  
       - Categorical: "good", "bad"
