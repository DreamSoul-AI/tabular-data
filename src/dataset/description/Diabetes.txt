Dataset Information
This dataset originates from the National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases. It focuses on a subset of female patients, at least 21 years old, of Pima Indian heritage, residing near Phoenix, Arizona, USA. The goal of the dataset is to predict whether a patient has diabetes based on various diagnostic measurements.
The outcome variable is binary, where:
1 indicates the patient has diabetes (based on World Health Organization criteria: 2-hour post-load plasma glucose ≥ 200 mg/dl at any survey examination or during routine medical care).
0 indicates the patient does not have diabetes.
In the original study, the ADAP learning algorithm was employed to forecast the onset of diabetes. The algorithm generates a real-valued prediction between 0 and 1, which is transformed into a binary decision using a cutoff value of 0.448. With 576 training instances, the algorithm achieved a sensitivity and specificity of 76% when tested on the remaining 192 instances.
